Questions

Which level should I start with?

Take the free level test above. If French is new to you, start with A0 Complete.

What do I get?

Two editions of every module. The PDF edition is print-ready, includes the answer key, and comes with the audio as MP3 files and a one-page memo. The web edition is completed online on this site, with self-checking answers and the audio built in. No app or subscription needed.

Is there audio?

Yes. The listening dialogue and the dictation of every module are recorded: as MP3 files with the PDF edition, and built into the page in the web edition.

Do I need a teacher?

No. Each pack includes the full answer key, a self-marking grid and a revision plan. It also works alongside lessons with any tutor.

Is a full level cheaper than its two parts?

Yes. A complete level costs less than its two parts. The Complete Method costs less than the four levels bought separately.

How can I pay?

The checkout shows only the methods available in your country of residence: bank card (Visa, Mastercard) and PayPal everywhere, charged in US dollars, plus the local methods of your country, such as mobile money, where we offer them. Your country is detected automatically; you can choose another one.

Is Module 1 really free?

Yes. Enter your email and A0 Module 1 arrives in your inbox: the PDF with its MP3 audio and the web edition. Nothing to pay, no card asked.

Are the DELF and TCF papers the official ones?

No. They are original practice papers written from scratch in the official format, to train under real conditions. DELF, DALF and TCF are diplomas and tests of France Éducation international; Pas à Pas French is independent.

How do live lessons work?

Choose a 30- or 60-minute session, or a pack of five, and propose at least 6 times over 3 days when you are free, in your own time zone. Olivier ticks the times that suit him; you receive an email, choose one of them and pay. Payment confirms the booking. At the time, come back to this page and press Join: the video lesson opens here, with a timer showing the time left. You can write to Olivier before booking, free of charge.

What do I need for a live lesson?

A computer or phone with a camera and a microphone, and a recent browser. When you join, the page checks your camera and microphone.

Can I get a refund?

The files are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, so sales are final. A faulty file is replaced, or refunded if it cannot be. If you are unsure which level to start with, take the level test, download Module 1 free, or write before buying. Refund policy.

I teach French on another platform. Can I buy?

Yes. Teachers are welcome to buy any product for their personal use: to study the method, prepare their lessons and teach their own students. The licence does not allow sharing, sending or posting the files; each learner who wants the files buys their own copy. Licence.

Are the reviews real?

Only buyers can leave a review, through a personal link sent after their order. Reviews are published automatically, good or bad; only insults, links and contact details are filtered out. Review rules.

What about B2, C1 and C2?

The B2, C1 and C2 courses are in preparation; B2 Part 1 is being written. Until then, the courses on sale go from A0 to B1. The DELF B2 and TCF Canada practice papers, which go up to B2, are already available.
