What's inside

Every module includes

  • Grammar explained in English, two points at most per module
  • A themed vocabulary list with English glosses
  • 9 or more exercises, with a full answer key
  • A listening dialogue and a dictation, both recorded
  • Two role-plays and homework
  • A graded test out of 20
  • A one-page memo card

Two editions

PDF edition: print-ready, answer key included, with the audio as MP3 files and a one-page memo PDF.

Web edition: completed online on this site, with self-checking answers and the audio built into the page.

Recurring characters

Léa, Thomas and Amina follow you from level to level, so every new point arrives in a familiar story.

Time per module

About four study sessions of forty minutes. Short, frequent sessions work better than one long one.

Questions

Which level should I start with?

Take the free level test above. If French is new to you, start with A0 Complete.

What do I get?

Two editions of every module. The PDF edition is print-ready, includes the answer key, and comes with the audio as MP3 files and a one-page memo. The web edition is completed online on this site, with self-checking answers and the audio built in. No app or subscription needed.

Is there audio?

Yes. The listening dialogue and the dictation of every module are recorded: as MP3 files with the PDF edition, and built into the page in the web edition.

Do I need a teacher?

No. Each pack includes the full answer key, a self-marking grid and a revision plan. It also works alongside lessons with any tutor.

Is a full level cheaper than its two parts?

Yes. A complete level costs $9 (4,900 FCFA) less than its two parts. The Complete Method costs $37 (20,300 FCFA) less than the four levels bought separately.

How can I pay?

Worldwide by bank card (Visa, Mastercard) or PayPal, charged in US dollars. In Cameroon, also by MTN MoMo or Orange Money, charged in FCFA. Online payment opens soon.

How do live lessons work?

Choose a 30- or 60-minute session, or a pack of five, and pick a time among Olivier's availability, shown in your own time zone. Payment confirms the booking. At the time, come back to this page and press Join: the video lesson opens here, with a timer showing the time left. You can write to Olivier before booking, free of charge.

What do I need for a live lesson?

A computer or phone with a camera and a microphone, and a recent browser. When you join, the page checks your camera and microphone.

Can I get a refund?

The files are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, so sales are final. If you are unsure which level to start with, take the level test or write before buying.

What about B2, C1 and C2?

They are in preparation. B2 Part 1 is being written. Nothing above B1 is on sale yet.
