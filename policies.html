5. Live lessons

  • Booking. You choose one of Olivier's free times, shown in your own time zone, from 3 hours ahead up to two weeks; the time is held for you while you pay, and payment confirms the lesson. If no time suits you, you can propose at least 3 start times when you are free (at most 3 a day, without overlapping); Olivier ticks the ones that suit him, you choose one and pay. You can talk with Olivier before booking, free of charge.
  • Where. The lesson takes place by video on this site. You need a camera, a microphone and a recent browser. Lessons are not recorded.
  • Rescheduling. You can move a lesson once, free of charge, if you ask at least 24 hours before it starts.
  • Late cancellation or absence. A lesson cancelled less than 24 hours before, or missed, counts as given. If you arrive late, the lesson still ends at the planned time.
  • If Olivier cancels, or the lesson cannot take place because of a problem on our side, you choose between a new time and a full refund of that lesson.
  • Packs of 5 lessons are valid for 2 months from the purchase. Lessons not booked within that time are lost.
  • Plans. A plan (4, 8 or 16 lessons of 60 minutes) runs for periods of 2 months. You book its lessons as you go within each period; lessons not booked by the end of a period do not carry over. At the end of each period the plan renews automatically, at the price shown when you subscribed, with a fresh set of lessons, until you cancel it. We email you 7 days before each renewal. You can cancel the renewal at any time before the renewal date, in one click from “My live lessons” or by writing to us: you keep the lessons left until the end of the current period, and nothing more is charged. If a renewal is charged and you have not booked any lesson of the new period, write to us within 7 days for a full refund of that renewal. With mobile money, each renewal is a payment request that you confirm on your phone.
  • Conduct. Lessons are a space of mutual respect. Olivier may end a lesson in case of abusive behaviour, without refund.
