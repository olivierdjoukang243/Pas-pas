  • Booking. You choose one of Olivier's open slots, shown in your own time zone, from 3 hours to one month ahead. The slot is held for you while you pay, and your payment confirms the lesson. With a bundle or a plan, you book the following lessons yourself in “My courses”, from the same open slots: one at a time, or every week or every month, within your period. You can talk to Olivier before booking, free of charge.
  • Where. Lessons take place by video on this site. You need a camera, a microphone and an up-to-date browser. Lessons are not recorded.
  • Changing a lesson. Up to 24 hours before it starts, you can change a lesson in “My courses”, free of charge: choose another time, or keep it for later in your account. Each lesson can be changed up to 2 times.
  • Late cancellation or no-show. If you cancel less than 24 hours before a lesson, or miss it, the lesson counts as taken. If you arrive late, the lesson still ends at the scheduled time.
  • If Olivier cancels, or the lesson can't take place because of a problem on our side, the lesson is put back in your account and you choose a new time. If your bundle or single lesson ends within a week, it is extended by one week to give you time to rebook. Lessons are not refunded.
  • Lessons in your account. A single lesson or a bundle of 5 lessons is valid for 2 months from the date of purchase; a plan's lessons, for the current period. A lesson waiting in your account (not booked yet, kept for later, or put back after a cancellation) must be booked before the end of that time. We email you a reminder 10 days before; lessons not booked by then are lost.
  • Plans. A plan (4, 8 or 16 lessons of 60 minutes) runs in 2-month periods, and you book your lessons as you go during each period. Lessons not booked by the end of a period don't carry over. Before paying, you choose how the plan renews for a new period with a fresh set of lessons, at the price shown when you subscribed: automatically (MTN MoMo or Orange Money only: on the renewal date, a payment request arrives on your phone and you approve it with your PIN; we remind you 7 days before) or by link (7 days before the end of each period, we email you a link to renew). Nothing is ever taken without your approval: if you don't approve the request or don't pay, the plan simply stops at the end of the period. You can switch between the two, or stop the renewal, in one click from “My courses” → Settings or by writing to us; you keep your remaining lessons until the end of the current period. If you renew and haven't booked any lessons in the new period, write to us within 5 business days for a full refund of that renewal.
