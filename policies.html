5. Live lessons

  • Booking. You propose at least 6 times over at least 3 days when you are free. Olivier ticks the times that suit him; you choose one of them and pay. Payment confirms the lesson. You can talk with Olivier before booking, free of charge.
  • Where. The lesson takes place by video on this site. You need a camera, a microphone and a recent browser. Lessons are not recorded.
  • Rescheduling. You can move a lesson once, free of charge, if you ask at least 24 hours before it starts.
  • Late cancellation or absence. A lesson cancelled less than 24 hours before, or missed, counts as given. If you arrive late, the lesson still ends at the planned time.
  • If Olivier cancels, or the lesson cannot take place because of a problem on our side, you choose between a new time and a full refund of that lesson.
  • Packs of 5 lessons are valid for 3 months from the purchase. Lessons not booked within that time are lost.
  • Conduct. Lessons are a space of mutual respect. Olivier may end a lesson in case of abusive behaviour, without refund.

The DELF and TCF Canada papers sold here are original practice papers written in the official format. They are not official papers and are not endorsed by France Éducation international, which owns the DELF, DALF and TCF. They help you train; no result at the real exam can be guaranteed.

7. Reviews

  • Who can review. Only buyers. After each purchase you receive an email with a personal link; each order gives one review.
  • Publication is automatic. Reviews are published at once, without being read or chosen by us. Every rating is published, from 1 to 5 stars: negative reviews are never hidden or removed because they are negative.
  • The only filter concerns the wording: insults or obscenities, links, email addresses and phone numbers, reviews written entirely in capitals, and texts shorter than 20 or longer than 1,200 characters. A review stopped by the filter is not published, and its author is told why and can correct it.
  • Nothing is offered in exchange for a review, positive or negative.
  • A review is shown with the author's first name and country only, with their agreement. Olivier may reply publicly under a review. You can ask us at any time to delete your review.

8. Privacy

What we collect, and why.

  • Your email address and name: to deliver your files or the free module, confirm your lessons and answer you.
  • Your country of residence: to show your currency and the payment methods of your country.
  • Your mobile money number (MTN MoMo, Orange Money): only to process that payment.
  • Your lesson requests (proposed times, topic, level): to organise your lessons.
  • Your review, if you leave one.
  • Marketing emails (French tips and news) are sent only if you tick the box for them. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Who processes your data for us. Our payment provider (Notch Pay) for payments; Google (Gmail) for sending emails; 8x8 (Jitsi as a Service) for the video lessons; geojs.io, which receives your IP address to find your country; Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to display the fonts; and the host of the site. We never sell your data.

How long. Order records are kept as long as accounting rules require. Marketing contacts are deleted when you unsubscribe or after 3 years without any contact. Lesson requests that are not booked are deleted after 3 months.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and object to its use, by writing to us. You may also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Age. Learners under 16 need the agreement of a parent or guardian to buy or book.

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps, on your own device, your chosen currency and level, your free-test settings and your booked lessons, so that the page remembers them. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.
